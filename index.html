Search and watch the web.

No ads, no directory spam, no AI slop. Find clean answers, save them with your family, and let your Mac keep an eye on the pages that matter.

Try

New here? Cleanweb does three things.

1. Searches the web with ads, AI fluff and directory spam filtered out — type above to try it.

2. Watches pages for you — drop in a price, an "out of stock" notice, an event listing or a social-media page, and your Mac checks on a schedule and pings you when something changes.

3. Drives a real Chrome on your Mac when you want to delegate something a single search can't answer — like "find the cheapest direct flight to Lisbon next weekend".
